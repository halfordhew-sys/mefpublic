About the Festival

The Manningtree Earth Festival is coordinated by volunteers from PACE Manningtree and delivered by passionate members of our community. We're bringing together knowledge, skills, and connections to help us all understand climate change, biodiversity loss, and the practical actions we can take.

🌡️Understanding the changing climate and the loss of biodiversity
🛠️Actions individuals, organisations and businesses can take

About & Supporters

PACE's Manningtree Earth Festival is coordinated by volunteers from PACE Manningtree — a registered charity bringing together knowledge, skills and connections to help our community respond to climate change and biodiversity loss.

Our Volunteers

The Manningtree Earth Festival is run entirely by volunteers. The festival is coordinated by the team at PACE Manningtree, and most of the individual events are organised and delivered by community members who have given their time freely. Most businesses and presenters involved have also donated their services to help keep the vast majority of events entirely free to attend.

If you've enjoyed the festival and would like to help PACE continue and develop more local projects, donations are very welcome. Please visit www.pacemanningtree.org.uk to find out more.
